About Me 关于我

欢迎来到我的主页。我是 Steven Zhang，一名来自中国的 XMUM（厦门大学马来西亚分校）学生。 Welcome to my archive. I'm Steven Zhang, a student from China currently studying at XMUM (Xiamen University Malaysia).

建立这个网站的初衷，是希望能有一个纯粹的角落，去安放我在旅途中捕获的光影切片。对我而言，相机不仅是一台记录仪，更是一台能够让时间倒流、空间重构的时光机。它帮我定格住那些稍纵即逝的瞬间，成为我凝视世界的方式。 The original intention of building this website was to create a dedicated sanctuary for the slices of light and shadow captured during my journeys. For me, a camera is not just a recording device; it is a time machine that allows time to reverse and space to reconstruct, freezing fleeting moments as my unique way of gazing at the world.

除了行旅的光影，我也会在这里分享日常的碎屑与一些随笔。如果你在这些影像与文字中找到了共鸣，非常欢迎通过社交媒体与我取得联系，期待与你相遇。 Beyond travel photography, I also share daily life fragments and occasional essays here. If you find resonance in my content, please feel free to connect with me via social media. I look forward to hearing from you.
